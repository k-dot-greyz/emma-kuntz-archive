Description:
Among Kunz's relatively rare figurative works, Work No. 086 depicts male and female forms embedded within a cosmic lattice of geometric lines. The human couple is integrated into the energy field rather than placed outside it — consistent with Kunz's view that human relationships exist within universal harmonic structures.

Serpentine 2019 Context:
Selected for the Serpentine exhibition as an example of Kunz's occasional departure from pure abstraction into figurative cosmic representation. Demonstrates the range of her visual vocabulary beyond concentric circles and cross motifs.
