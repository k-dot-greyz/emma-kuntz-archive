Description:
Work No. 094 is part of the permanent collection at the Emma Kunz Museum in Würenlos, displayed alongside the AION A grotto. The drawing represents Kunz's standard radiesthetic method applied to a mid-scale graph paper composition.

Institutional Context:
The Emma Kunz Museum opened adjacent to the Zentrum to provide public access to Kunz's work and the AION A healing site. Permanent collection works like No. 094 are typically not loaned for international exhibitions, making this image a valuable document of the on-site collection.
