Description:
Work No. 396 displays kaleidoscopic color modulation with pulsating concentric patterns radiating from a central axis. The drawing demonstrates Kunz's mastery of the radiesthetic method at scale — the pendulum guided both structural geometry and chromatic placement across the graph paper grid.

Serpentine 2019 Context:
Included in the Serpentine press materials and online documentation for the 2019 exhibition. The high-resolution press image (1001×1001) is among the best publicly available reproductions of this work.
