Description:
One of Kunz's most celebrated drawings, Work No. 012 presents a vertical cosmic diagram mapping the human condition. The vertical axis represents the path to enlightenment; horizontal bands structure zones of earthly and spiritual existence. Kunz created this during her mature period using radiesthesia — a divining pendulum guided the placement of every line on blue-lined graph paper.

Serpentine 2019 Context:
Featured as a highlight of the first UK solo exhibition of Emma Kunz's work. Curator Melissa Blanchflower selected this drawing for its clarity in communicating Kunz's cosmological vision. Reproduced in the Serpentine catalogue with essays by Dawn Ades, Hans Ulrich Obrist, and Ali Smith.
