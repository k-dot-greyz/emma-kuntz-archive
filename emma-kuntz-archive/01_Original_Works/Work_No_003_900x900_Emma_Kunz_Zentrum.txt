Description:
An early large-scale radiesthesia drawing from Kunz's mature period. Work No. 003 demonstrates the foundational method she applied to all subsequent works: the divining pendulum plots structural lines on graph paper, which Kunz then fills with coloured pencil in sessions lasting up to 24 hours.

Serpentine 2019 Context:
Included in the Serpentine 2019 selection as an introductory example of Kunz's drawing method. The relatively compact composition makes the radiesthetic grid structure clearly visible.
