Description:
Work No. 004 presents a complex mandala-like radiating grid — concentric geometric layers expanding outward from a central point. The drawing exemplifies Kunz's early large-scale period (post-1938) when she transitioned from exercise-book sketches to full-scale graph paper compositions guided by the pendulum.

Serpentine 2019 Context:
Used in Serpentine press photography and the AION A workshop promotional materials. One of the most visually accessible introductions to Kunz's radiesthetic drawing method.
