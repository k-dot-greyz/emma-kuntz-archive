# Related Projects & Institutional Context

This document maps the exhibitions, institutions, publications, and contemporary dialogues that surround Emma Kunz's work — providing context for the artworks catalogued in this archive.

## Primary Institutions

### Emma Kunz Zentrum / Foundation
- **Location**: Würenlos, Canton of Aargau, Switzerland
- **Founded**: 1986 by Anton C. Meier (Kunz's relative and estate guardian)
- **Holdings**: Complete 581-work catalogue raisonné, AION A grotto, healing rock quarry
- **Website**: https://www.emma-kunz.com/
- **Contact**: stiftung@emma-kunz.com
- **Online catalogue**: https://shop.emma-kunz.com/E/6/work-catalogue.htm

The Zentrum is the authoritative source for work identification, series classification (Kreis/Kreuz/Komplex), and reproduction permissions.

### Aargauer Kunsthaus
- **Location**: Aarau, Switzerland
- **Collection**: 500+ Emma Kunz works — the largest institutional holding
- **First exhibition**: *Der Fall Emma Kunz* (1973–74), curated by Harald Szeemann
- **Recent exhibition**: *Emma Kunz Cosmos — A Visionary in Dialogue with Contemporary Art* (2021)
- **Online catalogue**: https://aargauerkunsthaus.ch/en/exhibitions/online-catalogue/?filter=kuenstler_emma-kunz-penta9450

### Emma Kunz Museum
- **Location**: Würenlos (adjacent to Zentrum)
- **Focus**: Permanent collection display, visitor access to the AION A grotto

## Major Exhibitions

### Serpentine Galleries — *Visionary Drawings* (2019)
→ See [detailed exhibition document](serpentine_2019_exhibition.md)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Dates | 23 March – 19 May 2019 |
| Venue | Serpentine Gallery, London |
| Tour | Muzeum Susch, Switzerland (26 Jul – 10 Nov 2019) |
| Curator | Melissa Blanchflower |
| Conceived with | Christodoulos Panayiotou |
| Works shown | ~65 drawings |
| Significance | First UK solo exhibition; first solo show since the 1970s |

**Collaborative elements**: Panayiotou produced stone benches quarried from AION A rock, installed in the gallery. An accompanying workshop on AION A healing practice was led by homeopath Jacqueline G. Schaerer.

**Catalogue contributors**: Dawn Ades, Bice Curiger, Desmond Morris, Hans Ulrich Obrist, Tabita Rezaire, Ali Smith. Design: Roland Brauchli.

### Aargauer Kunsthaus — *Emma Kunz Cosmos* (2021)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Dates | 2021 |
| Venue | Aargauer Kunsthaus, Aarau |
| Editor | Yasmin Afschar |
| Concept | Dialogue between Kunz's drawings and contemporary artists |

Featured contemporary artists in dialogue with Kunz's work included Dora Budor (whose work was paired with Komplex-114). Works Werk Nr. 057 and Werk Nr. 101 from this archive were exhibition highlights.
