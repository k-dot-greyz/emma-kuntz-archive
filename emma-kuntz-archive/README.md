# Emma Kunz Digital Archive

**Version**: 2.0 (re-hydrated)  
**Last Updated**: August 21, 2026

This archive preserves and contextualizes the work of Emma Kunz — a Swiss healer, researcher, and artist who created over 400 pendulum-guided geometric drawings on graph paper between 1938 and 1963. Kunz considered herself primarily a healer; her drawings were diagnostic instruments, not artworks in the conventional sense. Posthumous exhibitions have repositioned her practice at the intersection of art, spirituality, and alternative medicine.

## Catalogue Raisonné Structure

Kunz's complete oeuvre (581 works) is organized into three series:

| Series | Range | Count |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Kreis (Circle) | 001–092 | 92 |
| Kreuz (Cross) | 001–172 | 172 |
| Komplex (Complex) | 001–316 | 316 |

Works are typically untitled (*ohne Titel*) and identified by series + number (e.g. Kreis-506).
