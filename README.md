# Emma Kunz Digital Archive

A research-oriented digital archive of the Swiss healer, researcher, and artist **Emma Kunz** (1892–1963), with emphasis on her geometric radiesthetic drawings, institutional collections, and major exhibition history — especially the Serpentine Galleries' 2019 presentation *Visionary Drawings*.
